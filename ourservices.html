Our Services

Bringing Kenya’s heritage to life — digitally and in communities.

What We Offer

Digital Heritage Archives

Digitize and preserve photographs, oral histories and artifacts so they remain accessible for future generations.

Virtual Cultural Tours

Immersive 360° tours and guided storytelling experiences that connect learners with communities across Kenya.

Community Workshops

Hands-on sessions that teach artisans, performers and youth skills for cultural preservation and creative enterprise.

Research & Documentation

Field research and interviews led by cultural experts to record stories, practices and local histories.

Educational Programs

School outreach, learning packs and teacher resources that bring cultural knowledge into classrooms.

Cultural Consultancy

Advisory services for events, media, museums and projects to ensure accurate and respectful representation.

Events Support

Logistics, promotion and community liaison services to help festivals and exhibitions run smoothly.

Partner With Us

Work with us to promote heritage, co-create programs, or fund community projects. We value ethical partnerships that benefit local people.
